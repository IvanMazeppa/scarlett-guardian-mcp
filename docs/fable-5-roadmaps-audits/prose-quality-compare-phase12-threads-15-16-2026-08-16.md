## 7. Potential improvements / follow-ups (NOTES ONLY)

Prioritized, evidence-based, no implementation:

1. **Highest: Probe AGI-delay interiority without boardroom scaffolding.** Thread 15’s second dressing turn asked for delay-tactic internal tension and got protective/homologation readiness instead. Run 1–2 live turns that leave an *open* strategic hook (Benjamin names fear/time pressure, stops) — gold-standard geometry from Summary 4 — and check whether Scarlett originates or still soft-pedals.
2. **High: Intimate duplex under fully resolved care rituals.** Thread 15 shower-wash still largely mirrors Benjamin’s checklist before “My turn.” Watch whether Phase 2’s echo correction fires in live preflights on that shape; if not, the prose gap will recur whenever the operator writes tennis-match aftercare.
3. **High: De-confound Thread 16-style lead tests.** Repeat a professional scene with **weaker** OOC (“not hollow-passive” only; no “take the lead / 500 words / intercept him”) to see if lobby-threat-class origination survives without director scaffolding.
4. **Medium: Pronoun / POV hygiene after high-ensemble turns.** Thread 16 briefly drifted to third-person reference under long ensemble description; quick recovery after OOC, but worth watching when cast is dense.
5. **Medium: Verbosity vs texture.** Forced long boardroom reactions are cinematic but can crowd Scarlett’s *choice* with room-telemetry. Prefer open hooks over minimum-word mandates when measuring Phase 3 necessity.
6. **Lower (process): Archive hygiene.** Label archive `thread-12` ↔ diagnostics “Thread 13”; retain crisis morning as preflight JSON, not only as reconstructed docs, so future prose compares can cite the freeze turns directly.
7. **Phase 3 gate reminder (from proposal):** Do **not** re-render Intention / lead invitation until open-hook sessions fail to restore lead. Thread 16 alone does **not** prove Phase 3 is needed; Thread 15’s AGI soft-pedal is the better failure probe.
